3. Detailed Technical Implementation Work

3.1 Work Package C: Earning & Reward Economy Engine (`reward_economy_engine.py`)

Engineered the core security and economic defense layer protecting VELOOP's reward payout budget against farming, bot attacks, and runaway token inflation:

  • Rewarded Ad Watch Velocity Cap: Enforces a strict ceiling of maximum 20 rewarded ads per 24 hours per user. Validates video playback completion duration (minimum 28 seconds required for a 30-second ad) to prevent early closure spoofing.
  • Tap-and-Earn Bot & Autoclicker Defense: Implemented dual-layer heuristic clickstream analysis:
    • Frequency Limit: Flags any tapping speed exceeding 10 taps/second (superhuman frequency threshold).
    • Micro-Jitter Variance Analysis: Autoclickers and software scripts generate robotic intervals with near-zero standard deviation (<8ms). The algorithm flags mechanical regularity as bot behavior and triggers mandatory CAPTCHA challenges.
  • Hourly Earning Velocity Ceiling: Restricts total XP accumulation to a maximum of 1,000 XP/hour per account, preventing automated rapid reward harvesting.
  • Token Mint-to-Burn Economic Modeling: Implemented dynamic tracking of the token burn ratio (tokens withdrawn or spent / tokens minted). Evaluates the Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) against available cash reserves to prevent financial insolvency.

3.2 Production dbt Transformation Pipeline (`dbt_project/`)

Structured the enterprise data transformation warehouse under the industry-standard Medallion Architecture:

  • `stg_users.sql` (Silver Layer): Deduplicates user records using ROW_NUMBER() OVER(PARTITION BY user_id), caps future registration timestamps at current UTC time, clamps negative balances to zero via GREATEST(xp_balance, 0), and generates cryptographically salted SHA-256 PII hashes.
  • `stg_events.sql` (Silver Layer): Trims trailing whitespaces and enforces Title Case formatting on feature names, eliminating SQL aggregation fragmentation.
  • `dim_user_lifecycle.sql` (Gold Layer): Evaluates user activity recency and lifetime engagement to classify accounts into HIGHLY_ACTIVE_7D, MODERATELY_ACTIVE_30D, DORMANT_AT_RISK, CHURNED, or SUSPENDED.
  • `fact_wallet_transactions.sql` (Gold Layer): Double-entry financial ledger recording atomic debit and credit accounts with running balances and guaranteed 0.0000 reconciliation variance.
  • `fct_user_retention.sql` (Gold Layer): Computes weekly registration cohort retention rates across Day 0, Day 1, Day 7, Day 14, and Day 30 milestones.
  • `schema.yml`: Automated testing assertions enforcing unique and not_null constraints on primary keys.
