3. Real-Time Event Streaming & Telemetry Ingestion Engine

3.1 Telemetry Contract & Ingestion Boundary

To prevent dirty or malformed event records from polluting downstream Bronze/Silver data lakes, the EventSchemaValidator enforces non-negotiable contract validation at the ingestion boundary:

  • Mandatory Envelope Keys: event_id (UUIDv4), user_id (string), event_name, event_timestamp (ISO8601), payload (dictionary), and idempotency_key (SHA-256 hash).
  • 28 Production Events Taxonomy: Rejects any unrecognized or custom event action, ensuring 100% adherence to the Work Package F tracking plan.
  • Temporal Drift Tolerance: Rejects future timestamps exceeding 300 seconds (preventing client-clock manipulation) and events older than 7 days (preventing replay attacks).

3.2 Stream Buffer & Deduplication Architecture

Streaming Component Technical Mechanism Production Operational Guarantee
Partition Buffer Micro-batch buffering with configurable flush threshold (default: 100 events). Sub-millisecond ingestion (P50 < 0.05ms) with zero client blocking.
Idempotency Deduplication Sliding-window hash set (10,000 keys) checking SHA-256 digest of payload. Exactly-once processing semantics across network retries and webhook resends.
Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) Failed schema payloads routed to segregated DLQ with failure diagnostics. Zero data loss; invalid payloads preserved for forensic inspection without blocking buffer.

4. Multi-Factor Withdrawal Fraud Risk Scorer

4.1 Mathematical Formulation of Risk Score

The composite fraud risk score evaluates monetary payout vulnerability by combining five independent risk signals:

$$\text{Risk Score} = 0.15 \cdot S_{\text{age}} + 0.25 \cdot S_{\text{vel}} + 0.25 \cdot S_{\text{dev}} + 0.15 \cdot S_{\text{geo}} + 0.20 \cdot S_{\text{jitter}}$$

Feature Factor Weight Evaluation Logic Risk Scoring Output
Account Age 15% Days elapsed since user registration. <3d: 1.0 | 3–7d: 0.6 | 7–30d: 0.2 | >30d: 0.0
24h Payout Velocity 25% Withdrawal attempts within rolling 24 hours. ≥3 attempts: 1.0 | 2 attempts: 0.5 | 1 attempt: 0.0
Device Collision 25% Accounts sharing identical hardware identifier. ≥3 accounts: 1.0 | 2 accounts: 0.6 | 1 account: 0.0
IP Geolocation Hop 15% Mismatch between IP country & registration country. Mismatched / Fast Hop: 1.0 | Domestic Match: 0.0
Clickstream Micro-Jitter 20% Standard deviation of tap timing intervals. <8ms (Bot): 1.0 | 8–15ms: 0.5 | >15ms (Human): 0.0

4.2 Decision Boundaries & Automated Queue Routing

  • Low-Risk (< 0.30) → AUTO_APPROVE: Forwarded immediately to GATEWAY_DISPATCH_QUEUE for atomic ledger execution.
  • Medium-Risk (0.30 – 0.6999) → MANUAL_REVIEW: Placed in escrow hold and routed to COMPLIANCE_OPERATIONS_QUEUE for analyst verification.
  • High-Risk (≥ 0.70) → AUTO_REJECT: Transaction blocked, balance returned to escrow freeze, routed to SECURITY_LOCKOUT_QUEUE.
