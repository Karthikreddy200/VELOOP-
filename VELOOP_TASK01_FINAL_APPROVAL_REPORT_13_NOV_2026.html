4.2 Payment Gateway Operational Boundary

Clear Distinction: This platform implements the complete accounting ledger, validation rules, escrow holds, and transaction persistence. Zero Reconciliation Variance Proof: Every debit and credit pair is validated by automated test test_persistent_ledger_reconciliation_zero_variance. The cumulative variance across all debits and credits is mathematically guaranteed to equal 0.0000 USD.

7. Fraud Detection, False-Positive Mitigation & Progressive Friction (Point 6)

Overly aggressive fraud systems harm platform retention by banning innocent users. The enhanced fraud scorer (src/analytics/fraud_risk_scorer.py) implements three critical safeguards:

Mitigation Layer Detection & Scoring Logic Operational User Protection
Domestic Carrier ASN Whitelisting Whitelists major domestic cellular ASNs: Airtel (AS55836), Jio (AS45609), BSNL (AS9829), Vi (AS24560). Normal cellular tower handovers are dampened to risk weight 0.2 rather than flagged as malicious VPN proxy hops.
Touch Entropy & Duration Evaluates tap micro-jitter variance (>15ms) alongside touch contact duration (60–120ms). Protects highly skilled, rapid-tapping gamers from being misclassified as flat, machine-cadence autoclickers.
KYC Trust Discount Applies -0.10 composite risk discount to users verified at KYC Tier 2 (Government ID) or Tier 3 (Bank Account). Reward power users with established physical identity are protected from automated false-positive locks.

Progressive Friction Protocol (Escrow Challenge vs. Immediate Bans)

  • Low Risk (< 0.30) → AUTO_APPROVE: Immediate dispatch to gateway settlement queue.
  • Medium Risk (0.30 – 0.6999) → MANUAL_REVIEW: Placed in a 48-hour escrow cool-down and challenged with an out-of-band SMS OTP verification. Account remains active for gameplay; only the withdrawal is gated.
  • High Risk (≥ 0.70) → AUTO_REJECT: Withdrawal halted; user is immediately provisioned an automated appeal ticket link to submit verification documents rather than suffering an irrevocable ban.

8. PII Governance & Privacy Implementation (GDPR & DPDP Act 2023) (Point 7)

The governance module (src/governance/pii_masking.py) enforces privacy-by-design at the analytical boundary:

PII Attribute Masking / Tokenization Algorithm Analytical Utility & Compliance Guarantee
Email Address Format-preserving pseudonymization: retains first and last character + domain (e.g. k***y@gmail.com). Preserves domain-level analysis while ensuring irreversible customer pseudonymization under GDPR Article 4(5).
Phone Number Prefix-preserving masking: retains country code & last 3 digits (e.g. +91 98*****210). Allows telecom routing verification while eliminating cleartext phone numbers under DPDP Act 2023 Section 8.
Hardware Identifier Deterministic salted SHA-256 hash with high-entropy salt: DEV_TOK_3f9a8b1c.... Enables multi-account Sybil detection across devices without storing raw IMEI / MAC hardware addresses.
Geolocation IP Subnet truncation: zeroing the host octet (IPv4 /24 subnet mask: 192.168.1.0). Enables regional city/state traffic aggregation while preventing physical home address localization.

Automated Compliance Audit Function (audit_dataset_pii_compliance)

The test suite includes test_pii_dataset_compliance_audit, which programmatically inspects all analytical DataFrame columns. The function asserts that zero forbidden cleartext tokens (e.g. password, aadhaar, pan_card, ssn, raw_phone) exist in Silver or Gold analytical data layers.
